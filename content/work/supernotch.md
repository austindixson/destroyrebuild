**Super Notch is a Mac app by Austin Dixson.** It brings agent sessions, voice, and a floating HUD into the flow of your desktop work.

[Visit supernotch.ai ↗](https://www.supernotch.ai)

## Less finding the work. More getting to it.

Recognize a project in the notch, see its recent context, and return to a supported agent session in the tool where the work already lives. Keep your tools; shorten the way back into them.

## Help beside the work

- **Sessions:** find and resume supported live and saved agent sessions.
- **Floating HUD:** talk or type beside your work, using available window and page context.
- **Talk:** ask a question out loud from the notch.
- **Type:** dictate into the focused field on your Mac, then review and send the words yourself.

Super Notch works with Grok or OpenAI for chat and voice answers. Type dictation turns speech into text without spending model tokens. Window context depends on app support and permissions.

## From this workshop to your Mac

Super Notch is available for macOS 14 and newer. The product site has the actual app recording, current features, pricing, and download details.
